## Use H2 headings for each main section

Pinterest visitors arrive expecting the specific thing the Pin promised, so the
opening lines should confirm they are in the right place before anything else.
Keep sections short and scannable.

### H3 for sub-points within a section

Structure each idea as: the problem, why it happens in small spaces, then the
practical solution. Add a product recommendation only where it genuinely helps.

Inline images go in the body with standard Markdown or the Astro `<Image />`
component once the image components are built in a later step.
